FROM FINDING TO FINAL RECORD

A workflow everyone can understand

Each step records who acted, what changed and what evidence supports the decision.

  1. 1
    Report

    Describe the unsafe condition and capture evidence at the worksite.

  2. 2
    Assign

    Classify risk, set the deadline and notify the responsible team.

  3. 3
    Correct

    Submit the corrective-action description and close-out photo.

  4. 4
    Verify and close

    Approve or reject the evidence and retain the complete signed record.
